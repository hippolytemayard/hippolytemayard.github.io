Second Opinion 3D — CBCT pathology & anatomy

Pearl AI · 2025 – present

Role. Sole technical owner of the 3D CBCT pathology program since Feb 2026.

Scope. Detection of five pathologies and segmentation of eight anatomical structures on cone-beam CT. Regulatory evidence for FDA 510(k) submissions: validation protocol, locked test set with enforced train/test independence, performance reports, technical file.

Outcome. Pathology detection shipped commercially in Second Opinion 3D; general launch in September 2026. Pearl is the first dental-AI company cleared by the FDA for both 2D and 3D imaging: Second Opinion 3D received 510(k) clearance on 27 May 2025 for anatomical segmentation on CBCT.

PyTorch · MONAI · nnU-Net · AWS · MLflow · GitLab CI

Second Opinion — 2D caries detection

Pearl AI · 2025 – present

Role. Owner of the caries model on intra-oral and panoramic X-rays.

Scope. Reduced false positives at constant recall, by investigating real-world failure modes through systematic re-annotation of dismissed clinical cases and retraining the detection model. Operating-point selection for different clinical users, with per-class PR analysis and per-device robustness checks. Caries progression shipped on top.

Reach. 50,000+ clinicians, 23,000+ practices, 120+ countries.
