**Task.** Segment catheter and guidewire in X-ray fluoroscopy sequences and detect instrument–vessel-wall collisions, across three acquisition domains. Part of the [CATHACTION](https://cathaction.github.io/) challenge at MICCAI 2026.

**Why it matters for my work.** Thin-structure segmentation under heavy domain shift (device, site, contrast protocol) is the same failure mode I deal with on CBCT in production — only here the ground truth is public and the comparison is open.

**Status.** Submission made. Code, technical report and a write-up on what transferred between domains and what did not will be published here by mid-November 2026.
