Hippolyte Mayard is a Senior ML Scientist at [Pearl AI](https://www.hellopearl.com/), where he is the technical owner of the 3D CBCT pathology program — the first dental-AI product line cleared by the FDA for both 2D and 3D imaging — and the author of the company's 2D caries detection model, used by more than 50,000 clinicians in 120 countries.

His work sits at the point where computer-vision research becomes a regulated medical device: model and data design, operating-point selection for different clinical users, clinical validation on locked test sets, FDA 510(k) technical documentation, and production deployment. Before Pearl he was the founding deep-learning engineer at [Allisone.ai](https://www.allisone.ai/), where he built the first models of the product and contributed to the research that led to the company's €10M Series A, and a research engineer at [INRIA](https://team.inria.fr/epione/) (Epione) on cone-beam CT reconstruction.

He holds the MVA master's degree from ENS Paris-Saclay and the IASD master's degree from PSL, and teaches Deep Learning for Image Analysis at Université Paris Dauphine-PSL since 2022.
